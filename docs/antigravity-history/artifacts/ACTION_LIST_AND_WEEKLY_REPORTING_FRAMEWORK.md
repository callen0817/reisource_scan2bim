# Action List & Discrepancy Tracking Framework

**Document Lead**: ReiSource  
**Milestone Authority**: Reis Contracting (General Contractor)  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

The **Action List** replaces fragmented email exchanges and uncoordinated markups with a single, live, centralized register tracking constructability items, federated model deliverables, and design reference contradictions.

---

## 2. Action List Categories

### 2.1 Category A: Federated Model Completion Items
- Modeling of secondary rough openings, backing, and structural interfaces.
- Alignment of interior stud cores with structural grid lines.
- Wall decomposition into physical framing and finish skins.
- Concrete grade beam depressions and MEP underground penetration blockouts.

### 2.2 Category B: Design Reference Discrepancy Tracking
- Contradictions between 2D PDF architectural drawing sets and 3D Revit models.
- Missing dimensions or elevation benchmarks in design backgrounds.
- Discrepancies between structural steel framing schedules and architectural ceiling pockets.
- Inconsistent trade routing between mechanical ductwork and plumbing risers.

### 2.3 Category C: Specifications & Constructability Bottlenecks
- Unspecified material finishes, fire ratings, or partition types.
- Missing manufacturer cut sheets for motorized shade pockets or custom millwork.
- Incompatible penetrations through engineered timber beams (Spearhead).

---

## 3. Milestone Prioritization with Reis Contracting

Every open item in the Action List is tagged with a target **Construction Milestone** supplied by Reis Contracting:
- **Milestone 1**: Excavation & Foundation Concrete Pour
- **Milestone 2**: Structural Mass Timber & Steel Erection
- **Milestone 3**: Building Envelope & Weather Barrier Closure
- **Milestone 4**: MEP Rough-in & Overhead Coordination
- **Milestone 5**: Interior Finishes & Drywall Closure

Items impacting upcoming site mobilization are escalated to Critical Priority in weekly coordination meetings.
