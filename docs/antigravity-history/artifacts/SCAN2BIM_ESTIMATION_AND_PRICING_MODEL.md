## 2. VDC Coordination & Scope Multipliers

- **Level 1 (Full VDC Modeling & Federation)**: Baseline 100% production effort.

- **Level 3 (Shop Drawing Assistance & Record Modeling)**: Dedicated shop drawing production and fabrication support.
